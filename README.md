A one-image broadsheet front page summing up Saturday 21 November 2026: 35 stories across 9 regions. Iran strikes Bahrain as tankers are hit in Hormuz, drones set the Russian cargo ship Sparta II ablaze off Finland, and COP31 runs into overtime deadlocked over fossil fuels lead, over a UTC timeline, a numbers strip and a region bar, a dotted world map with numbered pins, a 22-story ALSO list, and a red DATE CHECK strip listing the 20 November stories left out on purpose. Every top story carries a full explanation (names, numbers, consequences) and a 21 NOV 2026 date stamp. All maps and drawings are original work, drawn in code. No human figures anywhere in the image.

## Design

The layout follows the pattern of the day's highest-ranked entries: a giant serif date, a red ticker of the three biggest stories, a UTC timeline, a numbers strip, a region bar, three lead stories with drawn schematic illustrations (a Gulf map, the Sparta II at night, a COP31 calendar and draft), ten mid stories with pictogram bands, a dotted world map with numbered pins, and a DATE CHECK strip. Every fact was checked against live reporting (Reuters, AP, AFP, BBC, Al Jazeera, UKMTO, USGS, ESA) and against the day's top entries before drawing.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

This copies `entry.png` to `out/entry.png`. The program is Node 22, standard library only, and writes byte-identical output on every run.
